❓ Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I start a session? Click "Start Session" on a pending booking.
  • What does the panic button do? It sends an immediate email and WhatsApp alert to your emergency contact with your live location.
  • How is my payment protected? Payments are held in escrow and released only after you complete the service.
  • How do I update my emergency contact? Go to Profile → Emergency Contact.

🛡️ Safety Reminder

Always keep your phone's location services enabled when using the platform. The panic button is for genuine emergencies only.
